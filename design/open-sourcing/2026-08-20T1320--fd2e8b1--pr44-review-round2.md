The first review round (`2026-08-20T1140`) found 27 items in the endpoint-contract

## The two regressions the fixes introduced

1. **A wedged queue disguised as a server outage.** Putting `ValueError` /
   `UnicodeError` into the transient set was wrong: transient **stops the drain**,
   so one record that cannot be encoded (a lone surrogate — `json.loads` accepts it,
   UTF-8 encoding never will) blocked every later learning behind it, forever,
   reporting the block as "the server is down". The previous behavior crashed
   visibly. **The fix made it quieter and worse.** Now such a value settles like a
   `400` (`UNSENDABLE`): retrying cannot help, so it must not hold the queue.
2. **A case-insensitive filesystem silently un-claimed the slot.** Replacing
   `is_file()` probes with a `{"token","ingest-url"} & set(names)` intersection made
   the claim case-sensitive, so on APFS — this machine — a slot holding
   `TOKEN`/`INGEST-URL` stopped being claimed and **silently resolved the legacy
   endpoint**. That is the exact silent-wrong-endpoint class the whole fix existed
   to kill, reintroduced through a different door. The two questions are now
   separated: `iterdir()` (consumed, because a lazy generator raises nothing) answers
   *can we look at all*, and `is_file()` answers *is it there*, so filesystem
   semantics decide presence.

## What the contract lens added (13, all verified before fixing)

- `invalid_base()` accepted a value with a netloc and **no hostname** — scheme plus
  port passed as a usable endpoint.
- A slot file that is not UTF-8 **raised** out of `transport_config` — the same
  crash class that had just been fixed for URLs, one layer up. Both slot reads and
  both legacy hook reads now catch `ValueError` alongside `OSError`, and name the file.
- `npm` force-includes a **`browser`** entry point (probed on 12.0.2, like the bin
  array in round 1) — that belongs to the one owner, `check-hygiene.derive_subjects`.
- The hygiene dependency loaded at **import** time, outside the named-failure guard,
  so a broken owner produced a traceback the gate could not report. Loaded inside
  the call now.
- Sampling statuses cannot prove "every other status is transient" — a `451`
  carve-out is invisible to any finite matrix. Replaced with a **structural**
  assertion: `classify_status` may not compare against a status literal outside the
  declared sets.
- The self-test left its scratch tree behind when a control failed.
- Two promises had no control at all: that a broken slot's reason **names the file**,
  and that the misconfigured and unconfigured skips **read differently to a user**
  (`print_upload_summary` was called by nothing).
- Docs: the budget bullet still promised a 5.0 s ceiling the next sentence retracted;
  the identity bullet still said "verbatim" after the server-facts bullet had been
  corrected; `learn/redact.py` carried the same false claim; and my own repair of
  `SURFACES.md` had produced `compose/assemble.py and compose/assemble.py merges`.

## One fix was reverted as over-reach

The reviewer noted that an **indented** leg guard would leave both scans of
`check-parity.sh` at once. Making both regexes indentation-tolerant then counted a
`for`-loop's inner conditional as a leg. The invariant I tried instead — every gate
invoked inside a block has a verified subject — is false: a leg's guard subject is
often a data file, not the script it runs (`domains.json` guards
`check-domains.py`). Catching this needs block-structure analysis, which grep cannot
decide, and the repo's own rule is that gating an undecidable rule teaches people to
route around the gate. Reverted, and disclosed in the file as a residual.

## Also disclosed, not fixed

- A regular file at `~/.config/agent-bios` now stops a working legacy transport

- `provenance.json` is a declared pack-time artifact, so its generated content is

- The mirror URL exemptions carry no line needle, because `ko/` is a translation —
