# Decision ids moved from position to content — the old→new map

Until this change an id was `D-<seq>`, the row's position in `decisions/decisions.jsonl`; from here it is `D-<date>-<hex>`, derived from the record's instant, session and summary (decision `D-20260816-a34213`, and the tool's docstring). Every dated record under `design/` written before this day names decisions by their old ids; those records are not edited (rule 2 of AGENTS.md), so this table is how such a reference resolves. `python3 decisions/record-decision.py --render` prints the ledger under the new ids.

On branches that appended after the split (context-budget, repo-charter-design) the same positional numbers named DIFFERENT records; their rows migrate to their own content ids when those branches run `--migrate` after rebasing, and nothing here needs re-mapping.
