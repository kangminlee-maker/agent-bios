# V1, fourth slice, first increment: the delivery owner, the host adapters and qualified routes

V1 is written in five slices; the fourth delivers a composed preparation to an actual session.
It lands in two increments (`D-20260927-c38ff9`). This record is the first: the owner of links,
delivery attempts, observation and activation, the adapters for Claude Code and Codex, and the
rule that decides whether a route is supported. The design is
[the 01:34 design record](2026-09-27T0134--0715475--v1-slice4-delivery-design.md). The second
increment probes the installed hosts for real and delivers to a real session on each. V1 is not
complete and no node is accepted by it.

## What was written

- `workenv/delivery.py`: `recipient.link.open`, `recipient.delivery.attempt`,
  `delivery.observe`. It knows no host.
- `workenv/roles.py`: `session.routing.activate`, with the projection of a preparation's
  Instructions units and the bodies it delivers.
- `workenv/hosts/`: one adapter per host (`claude_code.py`, `codex.py`) stated as data, and the
  rule that a route is supported only where the adapter declares it and the latest probe of that
  recipient's delivery on the host's name and version ran for real and worked
  (`D-20260927-d0925d`, `D-20260927-fe273a`).
- `workenv/guides/memory-use.md`: the guide every activated routing points at
  (`D-20260927-57bac5`).
- `workenv/storage.py`: layout 5 adds `links` and `deliveries`.
- The contracts: C12 gains `new_delivery`, `current_delivery`, `child_delivery` and
  `rehydrated_delivery` in the probe, profile and route outcome schemas, with three examples;
  C11 and C12 state the rule.
- The driver: `given_steps` in the executor, the `qualified_routes` feature that gives a case's
  delivery probes (`D-20260927-09f43d`), the `shipped_guide` feature, and the scripted owner's
  shipped guide.
- Seven scenario specs state the probes their deliveries rest on: N15-C11-POS (four routes),
  CMP-PINS, DEL-PERSONAL, CMP-UI, N12-C07-NEG, DEL-TEAM and N15-C11-NEG (the current route).
  N15-C11-POS, CMP-PINS, CMP-UI and DEL-TEAM carry the fact inside a sentence they already had,
  because their narratives sit near the 2000-character limit.

## What the frozen scenarios corrected

Running every scenario against the new owner corrected four of its first rules:

- **Activation without a link** is served, not refused: seven cases activate preparations composed
  for no link (`D-20260927-2ef038`). A session on another host than the preparation's link is
  `recipient_mismatch`, and every activation refusal recovers by a new request.
- **A refused attempt is recorded.** One answered with its attempt writes private state, and on a
  held link it is a delivery requested and not seen, which `delivery.observe` answers as
  `not_observed` with `delivery_unobserved` (`D-20260927-0b963d`).
- **The route code** for a recipient other than a child is C12's `route_unsupported`.
- **The projection view** is `executor`, as every scenario states.
