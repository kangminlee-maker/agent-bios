## Product purpose and user decisions that must survive

The product lets workers compose, share and inherit **Instructions, Domain knowledge
and Decision memory** so that they work in their Team's context. Team is the basic
sharing unit, not necessarily a company. The goal is efficiency and continuity with
local adaptation, not compulsory uniform outputs or Team control of personal methods.

| Area | Accepted requirement / boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Simplicity | Keep a small, robust, conceptually extensible structure. Omit low-impact mechanisms deliberately; record reasons and revisit conditions. Fix causes rather than accumulating special-case patches. |
| Scope | Repository, personal and Team each have separate Instructions, knowledge and decision collections: nine source positions. Source ownership, applicability, physical copy, effective view and delivered snapshot are distinct. |
| I/K composition | Default repository > personal > Team. Preserve lower non-overlaps, disabled selections and original history. A local content override needs no Team waiver; rights on protected source bytes actually reused remain. Disabling one source does not suppress all other scopes. |
| Decision use | No automatic scope/newest/LLM winner for incompatible applicable decisions. Ask in the **actual working CLI/app conversation at consumption time**, not the Studio/TUI management screen. History comparison itself needs no application choice. |
| Choice lifetime | User chooses application or bounded custom resolution and keep-until-change / ask-each-use. Bind principal, concern/conditions, source context, all relevant participants and change continuity. Unchosen participant or relevant-set changes invalidate choice and mode; unrelated changes/new sessions do not. Known A→B→A never resurrects consent. |
| Retry and no answer | Same logical-use retry/page/reconnect restores its question/answer. Changed basis means a new sealed question version. Cancel/decline/timeout/automatic reply is no answer; preserve pending_user for dependent use, continue independent work. Valid keep can work headlessly. |
| Consumption mechanism | One local owner for read/use state, with role-specific semantics and actual-host question/resume adapters. Prefer qualified native forms; otherwise native question/ordinary conversation with verified user-origin evidence. Agent-written consent or tool-execution approval is not this user decision. Hook is optional, with no universal hidden-reasoning interception claim. |
| Zero Instructions | Explicit memory activation supplies a compact runtime-owned usage contract even with no Instructions content selected. Selecting sources in management alone activates nothing; no implicit native-global edits or K/M promotion into high-authority instructions. |
| UI | Entry observes location and bounded past records; it does not know the current business task/target. No goal form or inferred intention. Design criteria such as options/selection/effects must not leak into product headings. Normal start can validate and dispatch in one concrete action; no obligatory preview-confirm-start wizard. |
| Knowledge | Durable and changing are maintenance properties of coherent documents/tables/models/questions, not two databases. Preserve period/conditions/exceptions/source/edition; offer a real reviewed package and addition/extension path. Reuse onto 8+1 lenses; no compulsory formal ontology for every source. |
| Memory/history | Immutable decisions/events preserve reasons, alternatives, exact premises, correction/withdrawal/replacement and parallel workstreams. Repository ADR, Team ADR and personal decisions have distinct homes. Commits do not establish unrecorded reasons. Cross-tool progress capture still needs a concrete writer/schema/cadence/sharing contract. |
| Intake | Supplied, authored, imported, learned and distilled knowledge/decisions retain typed provenance and destination authority. Capture creates a candidate; it is not automatic publication, acceptance or an always instruction. |
| Sharing | Local/P2P foundation is mandatory and works without GitHub. GitHub is recommended and initially selected where eligible, but optional/removable. Closed networks and one or two exchange windows per week are first-class conditions. Replication is not global blockchain consensus or instant remote revocation. |
| Identity/access | Personal/local work needs no product signup or synthetic Team. Stable owned person/Team/device identities permit optional Team Google/Slack/OIDC. Provider logout, local lock and local signout have different effects; supported offline use and explicit reentry remain possible. |
| Complete operations | Team creation and full CRUD/lifecycle, members/devices/rights, review/publication/adoption, advanced Studio authoring/search/import/export/deletion/recovery are included scope, not blanket follow-ups. Final GUI packaging remains evidence-driven. |
| Compatibility | Backward compatibility is explicitly waived. Preserve current work and explicitly selected data, stop old writers for controlled cutover and retain unknown outcomes. Do not build old aliases/mixed-version support just because current code has them. |

## Lessons and exclusions that prevent regressions

- Prose cannot enforce model behavior. Registered/discovered/delivered/read/used/
  understood are separate claims; parent delivery is not child delivery.
- Retained bytes do not prove a saved choice is current. Hooks do not observe all
  semantic use or erase old context. Restrict enforcement claims to owned boundaries.
- Restart, consistent local backup restore, peer reconstruction and controlled
  cutover are different recoveries. Preserve unique drafts/outbox/unknown requests.
- Status strings, document assertions and registered test IDs are not execution
  evidence. Bind actual subject bytes, artifacts and predecessor results. Synthetic
  evaluator tests are explicitly not product tests or runner qualification.
- Decentralized transport does not eliminate source ownership, trust/control,
  conditional finalization, retention or scope boundaries. Copy count is not approval.
- Do not resolve historic defects by editing completed dated records or silently
  rebinding hashes. Publish a full dated successor when target design changes.
- Avoid mandatory cloud/account, blockchain consensus, universal schema/graph engine,
  universal semantic conflict solver, blanket hook gate and compatibility machinery.
  Their exclusions have explicit revisit conditions in SSOT S13.
