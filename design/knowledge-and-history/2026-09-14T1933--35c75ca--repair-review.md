# Root-cause repair of the development acceptance contract

The three reviewed gaps are repaired in the successor design, specification,
execution graph, test catalog and author-side evaluator. Their product evidence
remains planned: no Team/K/M runtime, real runner qualification or local database
backup/restore was implemented or certified by this work. The previous reviewed
bundle and its failing evidence remain unchanged.

The purpose is continuity of shared Team work, including unique local work during
disconnection. A completed implementation task is insufficient if the actual Team
journey, execution authority or recovery of that work has never been proved.

## Cause and structural correction

XR01 and XR02 shared a modeling error: implementation tasks were executable
dependencies, while milestone evidence and runner qualification were separate
annotations. The prose required proof that the executable graph did not consume.
XR03 conflated different recovery claims under broad test families: restarting a
process or reconstructing shared objects from peers does not recover unique local
drafts and pending-operation state after the local database is lost.

Schema 2 therefore uses one DAG for build, package, verification and conditional
qualification nodes. The catalog independently declares required acceptance claims
and their cases/subjects; the graph must provide and consume them. No parallel
milestone scheduler or universal product workflow engine was added.

Package production and verification also now have distinct effects. P18 creates
the immutable candidate before P17; P17 and M4 inspect that exact candidate.
CAP-14 belongs to M4 after accepted P18. These nodes cannot repair product source
while claiming to verify unchanged input. Failed verification sends work back to
its implementation owner and invalidates affected evidence.

## Why subsequent edits cannot silently retain old acceptance

One predicate checks every accepted result: exact run/node/attempt, plan and
profile binding, canonical required cases, appropriate evidence class, current
subject fingerprints, accepted predecessor record digests and existing artifact
bytes. P01's artifact freezes the case registry; a later node cannot privately
change its test meaning. Unattended records additionally bind a valid current
runner fingerprint and its accepted R0 record.

Subject manifests come from the trusted coordinator/runner, including relevant
shared code and configuration. This evaluator checks supplied evidence consistency;
it is not an automatic dependency discoverer or protection against fabricated
observations by a dishonest operator. Actual R0 evidence must establish the
resolver/recorder integration. Unrelated Team changes need not invalidate an
independent personal journey; relevant shared changes invalidate their consumers.

Failed, pending, unknown or in-flight attempts are not automatically retried.
An in-flight attempt prevents an older passed record from hiding unresolved work.
Evidence and unique artifacts are retained when revalidation is needed. A later
runner qualification cannot rewrite the execution mode of earlier work.

## Closure and limits

XR01–XR03 are closed as the named planning/evidence-contract defects. Actual product
acceptance, R0 qualification and backup restoration remain required work in the
graph. The static checks prevent the demonstrated omissions and stale-evidence
acceptance; they do not certify whole-design completeness or usability.

No compatibility layer, mandatory account service, new distributed consensus,
generic scheduling service or transcript database was introduced. These mechanisms
do not address the identified causes; the SSOT's explicit exclusions and revisit
conditions remain in force. The 15:04 visualization remains labeled historical.
