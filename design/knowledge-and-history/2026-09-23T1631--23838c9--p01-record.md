## The defect this run found, before the review was spent

The first P01 attempt was evaluated before dispatching its review, and the evaluator answered
`unaccepted prerequisite P00` — the node accepted four hours earlier.

The cause: a **carried binding** is one an accepted record already holds, kept verbatim because
that record states its digest. P00 ran again this morning under the successor bundle, and that
run's binding differs from the 2026-09-20 one, because the fixture closure holds the bundle
members and the bundle moved. The registry went on carrying `fa28b513…` while the accepted
record holds `3316083d…`. Nothing in the repository could notice: `cases.py` checks that the row
digests to the digest it states, which is true of both, and **which run is the accepted one is
outside the checkout**. The P01 recorder wrote the emitted bindings into the run, overwrote
P00's, and unaccepted it.

Fixed in two places, and only one of them is the repository's:

- the registry now carries today's accepted binding (`23838c9`), recovered from the review
  packet's own copy of the run;
- the recorder **refuses** to overwrite a binding that a record in that run was measured
  against, naming the profile. That is where the question can be answered, because the run file
  is the only artifact that knows which binding was accepted.

The refusal was then planted and watched: a copy of the run with a different P00 binding is
refused by name.
