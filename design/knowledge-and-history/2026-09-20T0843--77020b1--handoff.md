## How a node gets its review from now on

1. The recorder writes a passed record to `<run>/<node>/pending-record.json` and leaves the
   attempt in `run.in_flight`.
2. `attach_review.py subject …` prints `review-subject: <hex>`. That line is the packet's first line.
3. Dispatch through the read-only wrapper to a provider that is not an author, with
   `REVIEW_RECEIPT_DIR` set; keep packet, result and receipt.
4. Decide each finding: `not-a-defect` or `deferred`, with a note. A fix changes bytes, so it
   means a new attempt and a new review, not a decision.
5. `attach_review.py attach …` publishes the record only if the evaluator then accepts the node.
