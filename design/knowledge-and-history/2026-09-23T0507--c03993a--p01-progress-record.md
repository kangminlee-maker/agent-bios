### One case a walk could not settle here

`N23-PROVIDER-POS` observes a live provider end to end. A registered provider
application, its permitted callback and a test identity belong to the run
environment, not to a fixture. U18 owns how a case declares that prerequisite, and
this case waits on it. Its scenario says what it requires.

### What the rounds changed in the contracts

Each round's repairs are in its phase report; three changed a contract rather than a
scenario, and they are the ones to re-derive before trusting a reading:

- Every C06 operation returning a `reader_result` may return `source_member`, and a
  C04 projection returns the bytes of each unit it delivers, so a delivered unit's
  `body_digest` names real text.
- A source path may begin with a dot (`.gitignore`, `.githooks/pre-commit`); a
  segment of only dots is still refused.
- C09 states C02's exception: a holder other than the device that issued a control
  permit comes to hold it only through a package.

### Three readings settled without new vocabulary

They are settled, and a finding that re-opens one is not wanted:

1. A person's answer is separated from the host's own automation by the host's
   measured configuration and the `capability_probe` of `user_event` that qualified
   its route. A `host_reply` names no origin; the runtime classifies it.
2. Offering an inspection's result as a use is a `reader.use.resume` naming a use no
   admission created, refused `use_not_admitted`.
3. A registered provider application belongs to the run environment (above).

### Two recurring classes became checks

The rounds kept re-finding the same two shapes, so each is now a check with its own
negative control rather than a sentence in the next round's brief:

- **The runner preflight** gained `applied_bytes_are_the_submission` and
  `run_identity_preserved` ([D-20260923-dee716](../../decisions/decisions.jsonl)).
- **The generator** gained `delivered()`: a step whose operation's row returns
  `source_member` must return the bytes, so every `body_digest` on a record it
  returns is the digest of a member that same step returns
  ([D-20260923-b15793](../../decisions/decisions.jsonl)). A digest a record merely
  states — a revision's member, a capture's inputs — is untouched; that distinction
  is what the fifth walk's residue turned on. Measured before landing: 56 delivering
  steps and 60 digests, already all joined, so it fixes the state rather than asking
  for work. With `delivered()` removed, a planted violation regenerates and passes
  clean, which is what the check closes.

### A trap this unit paid for twice

A walk's verdict can be a fact about the instrument. In the sixth walk one profile
returned 14 of 15 cases inexpressible; the cause was a sentence in the reviewers'
own `STATE.md`, read as demanding a byte fixture for every manifest member. The
sentence was corrected, `tree/STATE.md` refreshed with `__SHA__` re-substituted, and
the profile re-run alone: 15 of 15 expressible. When one profile fails as a block
and its neighbours do not, suspect the packet before the tree.

The other: after a contract edit, run the whole workenv test set. Adding
`source_member` to the C06 returns broke `test_contracts.py` while
`test_scenarios.py` stayed green.
