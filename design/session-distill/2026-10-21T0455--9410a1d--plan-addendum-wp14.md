## Growth is the only verdict; a gate that could not measure does not pass

Design §13.1 gives the gate two blocking conditions: the line count or the byte count of
`claude/CLAUDE.md` grows against the base. Those two are the gate's verdicts, and its contract
test compares the implemented growth branches with exactly that two-branch set.

Measurement comes before the verdict:

- A side on which the file is **absent** is measured as empty. A staged deletion is a reduction
  (exit 0, disclosed); a base revision that lacks the file, and a commit with the file on neither
  side, measure as empty too and pass.
- A side that **cannot be read** — git fails to resolve the base revision or to read the index —
  leaves the gate with no measurement. It then exits 2, naming the side it could not read, and
  the umbrella counts that as a failure.

The second case is not a third verdict. It applies `AGENTS.md`'s gate rule, "a check that cannot
fire is not a check": a gate that judged nothing must not report clean, the same rule that makes
`gates/check-package.sh` refuse a leg with an empty subject set. Its codes are asserted under
their own self-test assertion citing this record, apart from §13.1's growth set.
