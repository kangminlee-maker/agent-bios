## How the records are read

`stage.py <runs> --sizes 10,40,160 --R <declared> --rescore [--arms …]` gives the cells
(ratio-of-sums cost per item per arm, contrasts on paired blocks, the screen bit, level
against the comparator band; exit 1 when a cell is INCOMPLETE, by design).
`bounds.py <runs> --sizes 10,40,160 --R <declared> --rescore [--arms …]` is the
pre-registered bound algorithm (design rev 14): block bootstrap, 10,000 draws, seed
20260905 with the draw digest on the record, one-sided percentile bounds at 90% in
discovery, undefined draws counting against, same-level band = the reference arm's
per-run SD of defects per item. **This stage is discovery.** A cell that clears a
threshold is a *candidate*; a verdict comes only from confirmation runs on fresh blocks at
1 − 0.1/k, k the candidates carried into confirmation **on that host**. The primary cell
is M=40.

**The registered algorithm resamples the R matched blocks.** A contrast with fewer sound
paired blocks than its declared R is read — its bounds are reported over the blocks it
has — but its label is qualified INCOMPLETE and counted apart, as the aggregator calls
the cell INCOMPLETE; a block that ran twice in one arm, which the aggregator reports and
calls incomplete, the reader refuses by name rather than reading the last copy. The reader did not do this when this record was first written
(*Corrections*, round 1): it took no R and labelled candidates on incomplete cells.

Two reader defects were found on first use, both fixed with a control whose revert fails
by name: `cells()` compared a contrast's paired-block count against the whole per-size R
dict, and the single-arm S9 control never reached the comparison (`2dc3404`); and
`bounds.py` took no R at all (the commit after this record). Self-test 255 → 259.

## What this cannot say

- **Nothing about quality.** Level is 0 on all 259 sound runs. The L2 task, at every M,
  is solved by every seat without a held-out defect; the same-level predicate holds
  vacuously and the band is 0. A KEEP or REBIND candidate here is a cost ratio on work
  the cheap seat does perfectly; it says nothing about the seat on work it gets wrong.
  L1 and L3 are not built (the generator draws L2 only) — until they are, no candidate
  can become a binding decision.
- **Nothing about the shipped binding.** The efforts are fixed by design
  (`D-20260904-29fbdd`: workhorse xhigh, sweep max — on Codex; Claude's sweep seat takes no effort). The shipped Claude bindings are
  `claude-opus-5` at `medium` for the workhorse tier and `claude-haiku-4-5` at `low` for
  the sweep tier (`launch/agent-launch.toml`, `[hosts.claude.tiers.workhorse]` and
  `[hosts.claude.tiers.sweep]`, at `c743348`). The Claude retention candidate compares
  sonnet-5 at xhigh with opus-5 at xhigh; whether sonnet at xhigh beats opus at medium is
  a different contrast, not run.
- **Nothing beyond discovery, and on Codex not even that.** Bounds are one-sided 90% on
  3–13 paired blocks; the design's verdicts come from confirmation on fresh blocks at
  1 − 0.1/k; and every Codex contrast is short of its R.
