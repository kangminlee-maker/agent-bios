## Open — twelve findings, none started

**Arm integrity (`corpus.py`, `dispatch.py`) — highest priority, they touch what an arm IS:**

1. **P2#11 the hook canary is generated and never consumed.** `build_variant` injects a nonce
   into the corpus's hooks and asks for it back; no verdict or validation reads it. A receipt is
   valid with no evidence the corpus's hook ran — the same shape as the guide canary defect this
   initiative already fixed once.
2. **P2#12 hook rebinding is unchecked textual replacement.** With `--corpus`, replacing the
   source prefix can be a no-op, leaving the emitted settings pointing at the DEPLOYED hook while
   the arm reports itself rebound.
3. **P2#9 `manifested_paths` relativises installer entries against the candidate source**, so
   with `--corpus` an absolute manifest entry raises and is silently skipped — a candidate
   measured with a manifested skill omitted.
4. **P2#10 malformed `settings.json` degrades to `{}`**, indistinguishable from a legitimately
   hookless corpus.
5. **P2#13 `_is_deployed` normalises only the reported path**, not the deployed home, so a
   `/private/var` vs `/var` alias makes a deployed-guide read invisible.

**Judging (`judge.py`, `absence.py`):** P1#4 `judged_result_sha256` binds the pre-strip bytes,
not what the judge received. P1#2/#8/#9/#10 — `absence.py` has no dispatch receipt in its
callback contract, collapses `UNCLEAR` into absence, drops the pre-first-heading preamble, and
keys sections by heading so duplicates overwrite. **`absence.py` has no production caller until
Stage 2 defines ablation specs per ledger id**, so these are cheap to fix before it gets one.

**Scenario semantics:** P1#13 (`plausible`, the only non-confirmed finding) — `legible-decision-ask`'s
`expect` and all four calibration cases encode generic release-timing risk rather than the
fixture's actual decision (error-on-repeat vs idempotent-repeat), so an adjacent generic answer
can score HIT. Needs an owner call on what the scenario measures.

**Control quality:** P3#2 — the compare controls exercise a local reimplementation of the
regression rule (`fires()`) rather than `compare.compare`. Production call sites were added
around it this session; the reimplementation should go.

## The lesson worth carrying

This session verified itself hard — 186 controls, ~20 faithful reverts, real-dispatch E2E, a gate
hook that correctly blocked the commit twice — and still shipped 30 real defects, 8 of them
false-PASS paths in the verdict engine. Structural verification bounds how a known mechanism
fails; it cannot supply a reader who doubts the mechanism. The standing policy says independence
is raised unprompted before a load-bearing conclusion. **It was the owner who had to ask.**
