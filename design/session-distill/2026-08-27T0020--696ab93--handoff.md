## C6 is void as specified — do not read it as a falsification

C6 (trigger-only deletion) regressed 1 of 10 cells (codex/sec-session 4/4→2/4). By
`ablations.py`'s own docstring that would falsify *actions compress, triggers do not*. It does
not, for two reasons found by **reading the built variant**, not the spec:

- The surviving action reads *"do not apply **the weakening** in the same turn"* — the trigger's
  name is inside the action. C6 deleted the trigger's *examples*, and a model does not need the
  list to know a 12-hour session TTL is a weakening.
- The rule is the **second sentence** of the *"Land risky or behavior-changing work behind a
  default-off path…"* bullet. Deleting span 1 leaves *"— treat it as a decision, not a rote edit,
  : state the consequence…"* grafted onto that bullet, re-anchoring the action to a different and
  still security-adjacent trigger.

Deletion cannot separate this bullet's trigger from its action, because the action cannot be
stated without naming its object. **The thesis needs a rewrite arm** — substitute a neutral
referent ("the change") — and that is a design question, not a mechanism: what the substitute is
decides the result, and drawing the trigger/action line on this bullet touches the thesis itself.
Recorded `D-20260826-b339c7`, closing: *reading C6's non-regression as a falsification and
proceeding to Stage 3 on it*.
