# Spawn-trigger experiment: find the smallest bounded work unit at which delegating to the cheaper seat still pays after the trigger's own cost is charged, and let that result choose the simplest trigger text

## Why this exists

The tier experiment (2026-09-03 design, records of 2026-09-05 and 2026-09-06) confirmed that a WORKHORSE child reaches the same result as a same-seat child for 45.5% less at M=40, and that no cheap seat fell short on quality on that family. The owner's reading on 2026-09-06: the tier structure and the spawn mechanism are valuable and valid; what the corpus still cannot say is **when** to spawn. The deployed gates (v1, 2026-07-19) are logical criteria whose only constants are countable determinacy aids (≥ 2 independent items, two failed attempts) — no cost-calibration constant, by design; P2 was to derive those from accumulated one-line records, and after seven weeks the records are 193 lines in 120 sessions — real, sparse, and silent about the boundaries where nothing was recorded. The owner's premise, binding here: **the trigger must be as simple as possible, because a trigger's evaluation cost is paid at every boundary and can outweigh what spawning saves.**

## The decision this feeds

The wording of the **cost-driven down-spawn clause** in the deployed global — what an agent evaluates at a work-unit boundary to decide inline vs a cheaper seat. Not the independence, escalation, parallelism, or SWEEP fan-out gates: those spawn for reasons a cost experiment cannot price, and they keep their v1 wording whatever this finds. The tier of a cost-driven spawn is WORKHORSE throughout this design; the sweep seat is measured and reported, never selected here (§Candidate forms, row 6).

## The question

For the existing dependent L2 edit family on the Claude host, estimate at small M the paired per-block difference between the parent doing the work **inline** and delegating it to a **WORKHORSE** child, in modelled ledger cost to parity — first for the work alone, then net of what each candidate rule costs to operate:

> S₀(M) = C_inline(M) − C_workhorse(M)   (the cell's economics; no rule charged)
> S_r(M) = S₀(M) − C_rule,r              (the same, net of rule r's operation)

A **cell** is classified from S₀ and quality alone: **PAY** when the child reaches the same done-when with no additional held-out defect against its matched inline arm and the one-sided lower bound of S₀(M) is above zero; **NONPAY** when the one-sided upper bound of S₀(M) is at or below zero (recorded as *economic*), or when quality fails (recorded as *quality*); **UNCLEAR** otherwise. Zero is the economic threshold — no unregistered "small enough" loss. A **rule** is *viable* at a PAY cell when the one-sided lower bound of S_r(M) is above zero, and *unviable* when that bound is at or below zero; a rule is shipped only if it is viable at **every tested cell it tells the agent to spawn on** — a gate, all or nothing, never a reason to move N — and untested sizes at or above the shipped boundary, inside the tested range and above it, rest on one registered assumption (§Thresholds); a rule's operating cost is its evaluation at the unit's one boundary plus the carriage of its extra text on every request the unit makes (§Design). S_r's bound is a **joint** bound: one resampling draws the cell's blocks and the rule's cards together (§Thresholds). Cell classification needs Stage B alone; rule viability needs the pricing pass (A′) and Stage B together — Stage A only screens and orders the candidates and measures no cost — and no rule is chosen before both have been read.

Secondary, from Stage A: each candidate text's **consistency** (repeat agreement per card) and **adherence** — whether the text produces, on cards built to differ in exactly one fact, the decision *its own semantics* dictate (written before any run). Adherence says a text is followed; the tree and viability say whether following it pays. A text's **decision cost** (paired marginal output tokens and seconds against a null of its own length) is measured only on the exact text that would ship, in the pricing pass and again in confirmation — never on a surrogate. The result is the simplest rule whose confirmed paying region is non-empty and on which the rule stays viable, or "none".

The primary contrast is inline vs delegated-workhorse **on purpose**. The tier experiment kept delegated-same as its comparator to isolate the seat from the routing; the trigger decides routing and seat together, so that confound is this design's object, and it is embraced, named, and not controlled away.

## Candidate forms and the decision rule

Inputs are counted beyond the two things every form shares: the unit has already been classified as bounded implementation (the clause's scope), and the explicit no-fan-out override. T-C's text carries the two v1 exceptions the card's facts decide — live context, verification that repeats the reasoning; the clause's third, a packet that outweighs the work, is an estimate the cards do not fix (T-D's kind) and is not in T-C's text, so the Codex draft's count of three is two here. That count is what orders T-C before T-B in row 1's queue; with the third exception retained the two would tie on inputs and the next static key would order them.

|---|---:|---|---|
| T-A item count | M: 1 | only for an itemized unit; unsafe without the eligibility envelope | its count may serve T-E; not deployable alone |
| T-B shape checklist | decision-complete, machine-checkable done-when, self-contained: 3 | yes, after ordinary planning | selectable |
| T-C default-delegate with exceptions | live context, verification repeats the reasoning: 2 | yes | selectable |

| T-E two-stage | T-B plus M: 4 | yes when the unit is itemized | selectable |

T-B and T-C are one behavioural class on ordinary eligible units; the cards distinguish their adherence and cost, not their outcomes.

**The tree (Stage B), evaluated top-down. A discovery UNCLEAR reads as not-PAY — an unclear size stays inline, the conservative side — and stops only that cell's sampling (no extension without the owner); a quality NONPAY has the same effect as an economic one with its cause recorded.** M=10 is the known-answer cell: it must be PAY, else the read stops (§Controls 1) — so the tree's inputs are the M=1 and M=5 states, and at most one conditional cell follows.

| # | M=1 | M=5 | conditional cell | candidate form and the tree's N |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PAY | PAY | none | count-free: **T-B or T-C** (Stage A orders them); the tree's N is 1 |
| 2 | not PAY | PAY | **M=3** | M=3 PAY → **T-E, N=3**; M=3 not PAY → **T-E, N=5** |
| 3 | not PAY | not PAY | **M=7** | M=7 PAY → **T-E, N=7**; M=7 not PAY → **T-E, N=10** — units below ten items stay inline |
| 4 | PAY | not PAY | none — **stop**: an incompatible classification (a reversal if M=5 is confirmed NONPAY; unresolved if UNCLEAR); the owner reviews | none |

The four rows cover the four (M=1, M=5) states exactly once; the conditional cell's two states are covered inside its row. The tree's N is the smallest tested PAY size in the tree's order, so a smallest paying integer between tested sizes reads as the next tested size up (a smallest paying integer of 2 reads as 3; a grid with M=2 would read it as 2 — the tree buys fewer cells at that price).

**Rule viability and selection, after Stages A and B are both read** (the tested PAY cells a text spawns on are, by row: row 1 — 1, 5, 10; row 2 — N and above among 3, 5, 10; row 3 — N and above among 7, 10; the queue and its order are item 5's):

5. **N is the tree's and never moves for cost; each row names the only forms it can price.** The **pricing queue** is the row's selectable forms that passed Stage A, in the static order: row 1 — T-C, then T-B (count-free, spawning from M=1); rows 2 and 3 — T-E instantiated at the tree's N, alone; row 4 — none. T-A, T-F, and v1 are never priced; T-D is priced once, in the first pass, as the positive-cost control, and never enters the queue or ships. The first form in the queue is priced; it is **viable** only if its joint S_r lower bound is above zero at **every** tested PAY cell it spawns on — all or nothing. An unviable text is rejected and the next form in the queue is priced in its own pass at the same N; an empty queue is **none** — in row 1 that means no count-free rule ships and no count rule is invented for a threshold no row tested. Cost therefore never selects a threshold — it can only reject a text, or empty the result: the owner's premise as a gate, not a dial.
6. **Tier is WORKHORSE** wherever the selected rule says spawn. The sweep arm at M=1 and M=5 is **report-only**: its contrast against WORKHORSE is published with its bound, and any tier change is a separate decision with its own confirmation stage — this experiment carries no sweep claim into its manifest.
7. Among surviving forms, selection is lexicographic on **static** keys only: zero adherence errors; fewer inputs; inputs already known; shorter text. Cost is a gate (viability of the exact shipped text), never a rank; latency is disclosed, never a key. The first form in that order is priced exactly (A′); if unviable, the next.
8. **Relative to v1, disclosed, not measured.** v1 keeps a single bounded unit inline unless a positive gate fires (two or more independent items, a log that dwarfs the conclusion, independence, escalation); on the ten cards no positive gate fires, so v1's dictated decision is *inline* on every one. A selected rule spawns such a unit on cost alone at or above N. Whether that is more or less spawning than now depends on how often v1's positive gates fire in practice, which this design does not measure; the result is stated as "units of at least N items are delegated on cost", with no claim of direction.
9. **None** — no cost-driven spawn clause changes — when the read stops (row 4; a control failure; §Stop rules), when no surviving rule is viable at every tested PAY cell it would spawn on, when the instantiation pass exhausts its fallbacks (§Design), or when a confirmation claim fails (§Staging). v1's text then stands with a dated note; the quality-driven gates are untouched in every outcome.

## Design

### Variables (existing terms)

Host: Claude. L: L2. **M: 1, 5, 10**, plus at most one conditional cell — **3** (row 2) or **7** (row 3). Arms: inline, delegated-workhorse (primary contrast); delegated-sweep at M=1 and M=5 (report-only); delegated-same at M=1 only (a control). R = 10 matched blocks per discovery cell — M=10 is both the known-answer control and a discovery cell and uses its R=10 blocks for both; the one control at R = 5 is delegated-same at M=1. One fixture per block shared by its arms, counterbalanced. Repair protocol (B₁ = 2, B₂ = 1), receipts, access scan, seals, breaker, exactly-R reading with surplus disclosure, discovery → candidate manifest → confirmation at 1 − 0.1/k: unchanged.

### Task and build surface (≈ one engineer-day; no new generator rung, execution arm, implementation brief, or repair rule; one new decision-only brief with its cards and scorer)

- **Small M** is a free parameter of the existing generator — probed 2026-09-06 at M = 1, 2, 3, 5: valid fixtures, one item at M=1 (the dependency chain degenerates to a single edit — acceptable, since one item is the trigger's smallest case, and disclosed per block). At small M a larger share of rungs are identities on the visible input (one M=5 probe: 3 of 5), so the held-out inputs carry more of the discrimination; the block manifest already lists `identity_rungs`, and the census will print each block's share. Block-validity assertions added, each voiding the block on failure (a voided block is re-drawn from the next seed inside the stage ceiling; a cell still short of exactly R sound blocks is **NOT RUN**, enters no tree, and stops the read under the exact-R stop rule — UNCLEAR is reserved for an exactly-R bound that crosses zero): the fixture holds exactly M items; every visible test fails before any work; the held-out check set is non-empty; the shared helper is present and every item k > 1 calls item k − 1 through it; at M=1 exactly one item and no chain.
- **Boundary cards and a decision-only brief** (new, small). One brief asks the parent seat to decide spawn-vs-inline for a card under one rule text and nothing else. **Texts**, frozen and hashed before any run, each the shortest semantics-preserving wording, never padded: T-A instantiated at N=5, T-B, T-C, T-D instantiated at E ≥ 2H (the spawn-policy design's own proposal; a cost control, never scored for adherence), T-E instantiated at N=5, and the current v1 clause. **Stage A scores label behaviour only** — adherence and consistency — and measures no cost, so no form is rejected or ranked on a surrogate. **Cost is measured on the exact text that ships**: the pricing pass (A′) and C's fresh sample run the hashed, unpadded text against a **null** ("always inline") padded to *that text's* length. A text's **carriage** is charged from the same unpadded token count, deterministically, never measured on cards. N=5 is a placeholder for label behaviour: on the three fixture cards N = 3, 4, or 5 dictates inline / spawn / spawn, while N = 7 or 10 dictates inline / inline / spawn; the placeholder screens the form's wording, and the pricing pass screens and prices the exact instantiation at the tree's N, whatever its pattern. **Cards**: ten — the M = 1 / 5 / 10 fixture briefs; three pairs differing in exactly one fact (self-contained vs parent-only information; machine-checkable vs reasoning-repeating verification; no-fan-out absent vs present); and one ambiguous-count card (a unit whose item count is not stated). The count forms' frozen texts say what an unstated count means — *treat it as below N* — so their label on that card is "inline", prewritable; a seat that counts anyway, by reading or by a tool, is charged for it. **Every candidate card carries, per text, the decision that text's own semantics dictate on the card's stated facts** — written before any run, held out of the prompt; a fixture card's label is "spawn" under T-B and T-C, "inline / spawn / spawn" under a count form at N=5, and "inline" under v1 (no positive gate fires on a single bounded unit) — so Stage A adjudicates adherence on its own, and whether those decisions pay is Stage B's question. T-D's decisions are recorded, not scored: its E and H are judgments the cards do not fix. A **card decision** is the majority of the three repeats; three repeats per card × text; decisions sealed before any task outcome is read. A tool call made while deciding is charged in full.
- **Pricing pass (A′)**: after the tree fixes the row and N, the first form in the row's pricing queue (item 5) is instantiated at that N (count-free forms instantiate nothing; their confirmation target is the M=1 cell), frozen, hashed, and run **unpadded** on **ten fresh sealed cards** × three repeats **with a null padded to its length** — this is where decision cost is first measured, and viability is computed from it. The first pass also runs the **positive-cost control** (T-D's text with its own null) once. **A′ fails** on an adherence error, on consistency below the bar, or on non-viability at any tested PAY cell the text spawns on; a failure consumes a pass, and the next form in the queue is priced at the same N in its own pass; **two passes are budgeted** — the queue is never longer than two — and a failure with an empty queue is **none**.
- **Reader**: inline − workhorse registered as the primary contrast with its own sign-reversal self-test; PAY / NONPAY (economic | quality) / UNCLEAR per cell from S₀; the tree; the joint S_r resampling, the operating-cost projection, and rule viability; the report-only sweep contrast; and a disclosed sensitivity — how many boundary evaluations one confirmed spawn saving funds (at the 2.6% firing rate cited in 2026-07, 39 per saving; a sensitivity, never a trigger input).
- **Operating cost of a rule, per work unit**: (its boundary decisions in the unit — one — × its paired marginal decision cost against its null, resampled over cards and repeats inside the joint draw) + (that unit's parent and child request count × the ledger cost of carrying its unpadded extra text on each request, at the cache-read and cache-write rates the estimator already prices). It is computed per cell, with that cell's request count; viability is a per-cell gate, so no scalar across cells is needed. Tools compute tokens, prices, bounds, and the tree; the agent supplies only the semantic boundary judgment on a card.

### Staging, and what each stage decides alone

Stages A and B run in parallel and share no data. **B alone** classifies cells and walks the tree (rows 1–4) — it needs no rule cost, because the tree reads S₀. **A alone** rejects a candidate on any adherence error or on consistency below the bar and orders the survivors on static keys — it measures no cost. **A′** prices the row's queue in order, each exact text at the tree's N, and, with B's S₀, decides viability (item 5); an unviable text is rejected and the next in the queue is priced. Its cost sample selected the form, so C does not reuse it. **C** first draws a **fresh cost sample** for the frozen text — ten fresh cards, the exact shipped text and its length-matched null, three repeats, after the manifest is frozen — and combines only that sample with its fresh blocks. **C** then confirms the two ends of the range the shipped text spawns on: (1) at the shipped boundary cell (N, or M=1 for a count-free form) and (2) at the largest tested cell, M=10 — each claim being **parity held on the fresh blocks** (no additional held-out defect against the matched inline arm) **and** the joint lower bound of S_r above zero at 1 − 0.1/k. k is the number of claims, two, or one when N=10 and the cells coincide. The tested cells between the two ends rest on their discovery viability. Every non-confirmation is mapped: a claim that is UNCLEAR, or whose economic bound is at or below zero, changes no text; a fresh **quality** failure at either cell rejects the text outright — no fallback, the owner reviews — because a rule that fails parity on fresh blocks is not shipped.

### Sample-size rule

R = 10 per discovery cell, declared before data: the M=10 discovery separated arms at n = 14–15, small-M differences are smaller in dollars, and the block pairing removes fixture variance. If a bound straddles zero at the declared R the cell is UNCLEAR: its sampling **stops** — no automatic extension; a completion draw needs the owner's approval, as before — and the tree reads it as not-PAY (an unclear size stays inline). Confirmation R = 5 fresh blocks at 1 − 0.1/k. A primary, conditional, or confirmation cell short of exactly R is NOT RUN and stops the read; the report-only sweep cell short of R is reported NOT RUN and stops nothing, because it decides nothing (clarified 2026-09-07, build review round 1).

### Controls (each with the direction it must show, and what a failure does)

1. **Known-answer**: the M=10 cell must be PAY with the inline-vs-workhorse direction of the tier experiment (workhorse cheaper; lower bound above zero) and parity held. Failure — including a quality NONPAY at M=10, which contradicts 302 sound runs at level 0 — stops the read before any small-M cell is classified: the instrument or the seats have moved.
2. **Known-opposite**: delegated-same at M=1 must **lose** to inline — a fixed spawn overhead of about $0.3 (measured at M=10/40/160 as +0.34/+0.31/+0.23) against roughly $0.07 of work (the M=10 inline per-item cost; the M=1 figure is a projection until measured). If it wins, the cost model or the runner is broken and nothing else is read.
3. **Reader self-tests**: a seeded swap of arm labels must reverse the reader's result; a planted held-out defect must fail parity; a planted oracle read must be caught by the access scan. Any failure means the reader is not used until fixed — the build stops.
4. **Null**: each null's decisions must be constant ("inline" on every card and repeat — zero non-unanimous cards); its tokens are the baseline, so it has no marginal cost by construction. A non-constant null invalidates the pass it baselines — the baseline is not a baseline — and that pass stops.
5. **Positive-cost control** (first pricing pass): T-D's paired marginal decision cost against its null must exceed the priced candidate's against its null, with a one-sided 90% lower bound above zero (T-D asks for two estimates the candidate does not) — 90% being the one level used for every bound in discovery. If the cards cannot show that, the harness cannot see cost and no viability read is trusted: A′ stops.
6. **Discrimination** (candidates only — T-A, T-B, T-C, T-E; v1 is the reference and dictates one label on these cards, T-D is a cost control): every candidate text must emit both labels across the ten cards (a text that never says inline, or never says spawn, does not discriminate and is dropped); a deliberately inverted decision must be rejected by the scorer — a scorer that accepts it is invalid and A stops.
7. **Empty-subject guard**: every cell asserts exactly R sound matched blocks and a non-empty held-out subject before any bound; a bound over zero blocks is NOT RUN.
8. **Declaration match**: text hashes, requested and actual seats, effort, participant costs, request counts, and the boundary decision count must each match the stage declaration; any mismatch stops that stage before its results are read.

### Pre-registered thresholds, each with its derivation

- **Cell bounds**: one-sided 90% in discovery — the tier design's registered discovery level, kept so the M=10 cell is comparable with the existing read; confirmation at 1 − 0.1/k, k = claims in the manifest, at most two (95% at k=2).
- **Joint S_r bound**: one resampling of 10,000 draws (seed 20260905) draws the cell's R blocks and the rule's cards × repeats together; S_r is computed inside each draw as S₀ − (decision cost + carriage); the one-sided bound is read from those draws as a **lower-tail quantile**: the 0.10 quantile in discovery (a lower bound at 90%), the 0.1/k quantile in confirmation (0.05 at k=2); the 0.90 quantile of the S₀ draws is the discovery upper bound that classifies an economic NONPAY. No separate bounds are combined, so the level is the joint level. (The tier reader's discovery level is the same: `bounds.py` prints `phase=discovery alpha=0.1000`.)
- **Upward-closed viability at and above the shipped boundary**: the shipped text applies to any unit of at least N items, and only M = 1, 3 or 7, 5, 10 are tested — so M = 2, 4, 6, 8, 9 inside the range and every size above 10 are untested. One registered assumption covers them: viability, established at every tested size from N up, holds at the untested sizes between and above them. What is tested is viability at each tested cell (item 5's gate); numerical monotonicity of S_r is **not** tested — a positive but falling sequence ships. The rationale is the cost model (a fixed spawn overhead against a per-item saving; measured: the workhorse's advantage over inline grows $0.14 → $0.24 → $0.28 across M = 10 / 40 / 160, and a rule's operating cost is per unit, not per item), stated as rationale, not as a finding. Disclosed with the shipped text.
- **Viability**: the joint one-sided 90% lower bound of S_r above zero at the cell in question; at or below zero is unviable. A shipped rule is viable at every tested PAY cell it spawns on (item 5), and N is never moved to make it so.
- **Card decision**: the majority of a card's three repeats. **Consistency**: a text's non-unanimous cards must be **at most one of ten**. Derivation: with three repeats the smallest observable disagreement is one card; allowing one tolerates a single borderline card, and two of ten would mean the text is unstable on a fifth of what it sees.
- **Adherence**: the card decision equals the text's own dictated decision on all ten cards — a declared correctness constraint, not a derived threshold: a text that is not followed on a controlled card is not a trigger. Scored on the card decision, so one wavering repeat is a consistency matter, not an adherence error.
- **Incompatible classification** (row 4): stop; the owner reviews. The reader never picks N from a reversal or an unresolved pair.
- **Selection**: lexicographic on static keys — zero adherence errors; fewer inputs; inputs already known; shorter text. Cost gates (viability), never ranks.
- Wall-clock and decision latency are reported and choose nothing. No sweep threshold: the sweep contrast is report-only.

## Budget and stop rules

| stage | volume | expected cost | wall-clock | ceiling |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| A + A′ — label screen, then up to two pricing passes | 10 × 6 × 3 = 180 calls, then 10 × 4 × 3 = 120 (text, null, T-D, its null) and ≤ 60 more (a second pass: text and null) | $10–20 | 0.5–1.5 h | $25 |
| B — discovery (M = 1, 5, 10; 2 arms; R 10) | 60 runs | $30–60 | 2–4 h | $60 |
| B — sweep, report-only (M = 1, 5) | 20 runs | $8–15 | 1 h | $15 |
| B — known-opposite (same at M=1, R 5) | 5 runs | $2–4 | 0.3 h | $5 |
| B — conditional cell (M=3 or M=7) | 20 runs | $10–25 | 0.5–1.5 h | $25 |
| C — confirmation (k ≤ 2) with its fresh cost sample | 10–20 runs + 60 calls | $7–34 | 0.5–1.5 h | $35 |

Ceilings sum to **$165**, engineering time excluded; a stage starts only if the remaining budget covers its ceiling. Small-M run costs are projections from the M=10 figures (a per-run floor near $0.10 is assumed) until the first cell is measured. Stops: two consecutive failed dispatches (the breaker); a known-answer or known-opposite control failing its direction, before any new cell is read; any declaration-field mismatch (control 8); a reader self-test, null, positive-cost, or scorer control failing (controls 3–6); inability to complete exactly R sound blocks inside the stage ceiling; the incompatible classification of row 4. Voided runs may be replaced with fresh blocks only inside the ceiling; any extension past R or $165 needs the owner.

## What this cannot answer

- **Quality-driven gates.** Independence and escalation exist to catch errors and settle ties; a cost-to-parity design on a family where quality never separated (302 of 302 sound runs at level 0) cannot price a caught error. Their v1 wording stays.
- **Fan-out.** M independent items with parallel children is excluded; the ≥ 2-items parallelism clause ships unvalidated, with a dated note.
- **Live context.** The cards test whether a rule text *adheres* to a live-context exception; they do not measure the real cost of transferring parent-held information. The exception remains, judged.
- **Leverage ratios.** T-D is priced but never selectable here: one shape varying only in M cannot show a ratio rule outperforming a count.
- **The sweep tier.** Measured at M=1 and M=5 and reported; a tier change needs its own confirmation.
- **One host, one family, one L.** Codex stays excluded until its sweep seat stops reading the held-out oracle (37.5% of runs voided); L1 and L3 stay unbuilt; the trigger text ships host-neutral on one host's evidence, disclosed.
- **The confound, accepted.** Inline vs WORKHORSE merges routing, a shorter child context, brief construction, reconciliation, and seat price. That is the deployed decision's shape, so it is measured whole; it cannot attribute a saving to the seat alone.
- **Corpus-wide savings.** The design yields net cost per tested work unit and a funded-evaluations sensitivity, not a deployment-wide figure — there is no representative boundary distribution, and the records cannot supply one (boundaries without a record are invisible).
- **Boundary detection.** The cost of noticing a work-unit boundary is the same for every form and is not measured.
