## What this cannot say

- **One host, one family, one seat pair.** N = 5 is a constant of the dependent L2 edit family with claude-opus-5 at xhigh as parent and claude-sonnet-5 as child; the Codex host was not run (decision 5, default applied), and L1/L3 were not run (decision 4).
- **The decision cost is below the instrument's resolution.** Measured $0.0028–0.0032 per decision with a per-row spread larger than the mean; control 5 could not order two texts by it. The one-cent floor is a declaration. The margin that absorbs it: at the floor, M = 5 discovery viability holds up to a decision cost of about $0.020 (S₀'s 0.10 quantile $0.0215 less carriage), and confirmation at M = 5 to about $0.023 (its 0.05 quantile $0.0198 above the measured $0.0032).
- **The boundary is conservative.** M = 1 was NONPAY, M = 3 UNCLEAR at R = 10 (point estimate −$0.0024), and M = 2 and M = 4 were never tested: the smallest paying size may be 2, 3, 4 or 5. N = 5 is the smallest **tested** size shown to pay, which is what the tree selects by design (a smallest paying integer of 2 would read as 3 on a grid that tested 3).
- **Untested sizes rest on the registered assumption**: viability at every tested size from N up is read as viability at M = 6–9 and above 10 (upward-closed; the rationale is the cost model, not a finding).
