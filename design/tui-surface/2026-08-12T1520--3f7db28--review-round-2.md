# Round-two review: what the earlier handoff got wrong

The handoff at `5acd177` is left exactly as written. This record carries what two
reviewers found afterwards, including two claims that handoff makes which are false.
Read both; where they disagree, this one is later.

## Corrections to `2026-08-12T1450--5acd177--handoff.md`

**"The key scanner is single-line … a wrapped call reads as uncalled."** False. The
pattern is `t\(\s*"…"\s*\)` and `\s` matches newlines, so a call broken across lines
is seen normally. Only a COMPUTED key — `t(f"tier.{tier}…")` — is invisible. The
claim was inherited from the previous handoff, repeated into that one, into a commit
message and into the gate's docstring, and was never tested until a review tested it.

**"The only screen group still English."** Also false, and the scope it implies is
too small. Chrome and summaries are untranslated too: the numbered prompt line,
`Cancelled.`, `Launch? [Y/n/q]`, Textual panel titles and footer hints,
`setup_summary_lines`, the launch summary, plus preset labels and review-method
descriptions that come from config a user may own. A reviewer rated that mixture
High against the language menu's own promise that "런처 화면만 바뀝니다".

**Settle the chrome question before starting the review editor.** The two answers
give materially different work, and the handoff's figure of ~100 literals counts
only the review-editor group.

## One duplication the review editor inherits

`wizard.title` ("Register another reviewer" / "다른 리뷰어 등록") is a catalog key used
on the wizard's own screens, while the menu row that OPENS the wizard is a hardcoded
`"Register another reviewer…"` literal. They have already drifted by an ellipsis.
Resolving it means translating that row, which half-translates the review screen
unless the whole group moves at once — which is why it is recorded rather than fixed.

## What the round produced

Sixteen findings across two reviewers, largely disjoint: the empirical one found the
end-to-end leg passing while the deployed launcher was broken; the cross-family one
found the deploy-ordering rationale false and four holes in the catalog format gate.
The fixes and their controls are in PR #29; decisions **D-0062** (deploy order) and
the earlier **D-0059**/**D-0060** carry the reasoning.

Two things worth carrying forward as method rather than as facts:

- A guard that validates a shape field by field is a queue that refills. The corpus
  status guard blocked `summary` and `versions` and left `summary.placed_by_layer`,
  `domains.applied[]` and `last_apply.requested` killing the root menu. The readers
  degrade now instead of predicting the shape.
- A control that shares the subject's instrument can only find typos, and a control
  that does not ride the production path is not a control. Both were caught here:
  the panel checker borrowed the launcher's width function, and a counter meant to
  prove a loop ran was satisfied by a stubbed loop.
