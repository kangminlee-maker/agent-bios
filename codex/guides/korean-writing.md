# Writing in Korean

Before writing, revising, or translating Korean prose, read and apply this entire guide.
If the same complete text is already in the current task context, no duplicate file read is needed.
Apply it to responses, reports, notices, slide wording, and UI copy while respecting the requested
length, format, and register. Do not force a headline or lengthy evidence onto a short response.
Preserve quotations and identifiers that must remain exact.

First decide what the reader needs to understand or judge. Connect the supporting evidence and
conditions, and preserve conceptual distinctions and factual scope when shortening the text.
The Korean golden sentences below are hypothetical teaching examples, not real facts or fixed
templates. Apply their preservation of meaning, rather than copying sentence counts, headings,
or endings.

## 1. Define the question and central judgment

Make each fact, comparison, and explanation's role in the central judgment clear. Split independent
questions, or explain why they belong together.

- Avoid: “문의가 늘었다. 담당자는 세 명이다. 안내 문서를 개편했다.”
- Golden: “반복 문의를 줄이기 위해 안내 문서를 개편했다. 담당자 세 명이 자주 받는 질문을 모아 답변을 보강했다.”

Use this connection only when the purpose and work described in the golden are supported.
Do not invent purpose or activities from a list of facts alone.

## 2. Make the subject and central judgment visible in the headline

Avoid references that require earlier text or headlines that only preview a count. The argument
should connect when headlines are read alone. Do not force a conclusion onto an overview,
definition, or transition.

- Avoid: “세 가지 개선 사항”
- Golden: “신청 절차를 줄여 사용자의 입력 부담을 낮춘다”

For a definition, a role-revealing title such as “신청 자격의 정의” is also appropriate.

## 3. Keep claims within the evidence's scope and certainty

Distinguish hypotheses from confirmed facts, examples from actual selections, and temporal order
from causality. Retain uncertainty where the source has not established a relationship.

- Avoid: “안내 문서 개편으로 문의가 감소했다.”
- Golden: “안내 문서 개편 후 문의가 감소했다. 다만 같은 기간 이용자 수도 줄어, 개편의 효과인지는 확인되지 않았다.”

Mention the decline in users only when supported too. Do not invent another fact to explain an
unconfirmed cause.

## 4. Use the same name for the same concept and distinguish different concepts

Do not alternate synonyms merely for stylistic variety. Even when shortening an explanation,
retain each independent concept's name, definition, and difference from others.

- Avoid: “활성 사용자는 주간 이용자를 뜻한다. 참여 고객은 이번 주 120명이다.”
- Golden: “활성 사용자는 일주일 동안 한 번 이상 서비스를 이용한 사용자다. 이번 주 활성 사용자는 120명이다.”

Use the source or agreed definition. Ambiguous terminology does not authorize a new threshold.

## 5. Preserve the subject and action even in short wording

Qualify ambiguous words such as scope, criteria, or completion with the object the reader needs.
Do not fill gaps in compressed wording with meaning absent from the source.

- Avoid: “통과 범위와 보완 항목을 함께 남깁니다.”
- Golden: “검토를 통과한 범위와 보완할 항목을 함께 기록합니다.”

Name each state the reader must distinguish directly.

- Avoid: “0처럼 보이는 누락을 구분합니다.”
- Golden: “값이 누락된 경우와 실제 금액이 0인 경우를 구분합니다.”

## 6. State relationships between concepts explicitly

Make clear what causes, conditions, or forms part of what, and what is compared with what.
Do not add unsupported causality, sequence, or superiority to create a connection.

- Avoid: “교육 참여와 배포 권한은 연결됩니다.”
- Golden: “교육 이수는 배포 권한을 신청하기 위한 조건입니다. 교육을 이수해도 권한이 자동으로 부여되지는 않습니다.”

## 7. Move from judgment to evidence

Present the central judgment and necessary premises, then the supporting explanation. Distinguish
new implications derived from the explanation and avoid repeating the same content in several places.

- Avoid: “연동 시험 두 건이 남았다. 금요일에 시험 환경을 사용할 수 있다. 출시 일정 조정이 필요하다.”
- Golden: “출시를 다음 주로 미뤄야 한다. 필수 연동 시험 두 건이 남아 있으며, 시험 환경은 이번 주 금요일부터 사용할 수 있다.”

This example assumes the release timing and mandatory tests are established. Do not settle a
schedule or condition absent from the source.

## 8. Keep conditions, exceptions, and scope close to the claim

Do not relegate interpretation-changing conditions to incidental information. Align units, periods,
subjects, and denominators when comparing numbers; disclose differences in the comparison bases.

- Avoid: “모든 사용자는 신청을 취소할 수 있다.”
- Golden: “사용자는 승인 전까지 신청을 취소할 수 있다. 승인 후에는 담당자에게 취소를 요청해야 한다.”

## 9. Distinguish proposals, available actions, and completed states

Do not describe a proposed procedure as an implemented feature. Name what has completed and
keep review, approval, finalization, and transmission as distinct states.

- Avoid, on a proposal screen before implementation: “원천 자료부터 회계 시스템 입력용 집계까지 검토합니다.”
- Golden: “원천 자료부터 회계 시스템 입력용 집계까지, 검토 절차를 제안합니다.”

- Avoid: “검토가 완료되어 회계 처리가 끝났습니다.”
- Golden: “검토를 완료했습니다. 회계 승인과 결산 확정 여부는 별도로 확인해야 합니다.”

## 10. Match action copy to actual behavior

Buttons and links should say what the user will do or see. Distinguish viewing, selecting, saving,
and submitting. Do not promise a result that the click alone does not achieve.

- Avoid, on a button opening a scope explanation: “검토 시작”
- Golden: “검토 범위 안내 보기”

If the destination actually allows selection, use “검토 기간·상품 선택”.

## 11. Remove repetition while retaining necessary explanation

Do not delete essential evidence, definitions, or conditions for brevity. Do not add claims or
repeat statements to fill space. Separate sentences with different roles, such as definition
and interpretation.

- Avoid: “처리 시간을 단축하고 더 빠르게 처리하기 위해 중복 확인 절차를 없애 처리 속도를 개선한다.”
- Golden: “처리 시간을 줄이기 위해 같은 정보를 두 번 확인하는 절차를 한 번으로 합친다.”

## 12. Compare the finished text with the source and actual state

Check that key concepts, figures, conditions, subjects, and relationships survive. For features
and procedures, also verify available behavior and current state. The headlines and body should
communicate the argument without the author's additional explanation.

- Source: “시범 운영에 참여한 20개 팀 중 12개 팀이 다음 분기에도 사용할 의향이 있다고 답했다.”
- Avoid: “고객의 60%가 재계약을 확정했다.”
- Golden: “시범 운영에 참여한 20개 팀 중 12개 팀(60%)이 다음 분기에도 사용할 의향을 밝혔다.”

Preserve the subject, denominator, and response meaning when summarizing. Do not turn intent
to use into a confirmed renewal.
